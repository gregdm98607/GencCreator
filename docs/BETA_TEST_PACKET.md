# GenCreator Beta Test Packet

> **Status:** Ready to send
> **Last updated:** 2026-07-21
> **Sprint:** 8 — Launch Readiness + Beta Proof

## Purpose

Collect lightweight user evidence before Gate 2 to reduce the plan-quality risk accepted when Gate 1 behavioral validation was waived.

This is not a broad product-discovery round. It asks whether the deployed MVP gives beginner-to-intermediate genealogists a clear next action.

## Test Link

Primary:

`https://www.operationgrannyfiles.com/research-planner/`

Fallback:

`https://www.operationgrannyfiles.com/gencreator/`

## Who To Ask

Target 5-10 Operation Granny Files readers or genealogy-adjacent contacts who match at least one of these:

- Beginner-to-intermediate family historian.
- Has one ancestor or family question they are stuck on.
- Comfortable using a web form for 10-15 minutes.
- Willing to answer five short follow-up questions.

Avoid using only expert genealogists; they may judge the planner against advanced workflows rather than the MVP audience.

## Short Ask

Hi [Name] — quick ask. I’m testing a free genealogy research planner for Operation Granny Files.

You type in what you know about one ancestor, and it gives you a ranked list of what to look for next. It should take about 10-15 minutes.

Would you be willing to try it and answer five quick questions? If yes, use this link:

https://www.operationgrannyfiles.com/research-planner/

## Follow-Up Questions

Send these after the tester has generated a plan:

1. Did the plan give you at least one clear next action?
2. Were any recommendations confusing, too advanced, or irrelevant?
3. What would you actually click or do first?
4. What important record type or clue felt missing?
5. On a 1-5 scale, how useful was the plan?

Optional context question:

6. What ancestor or research problem did you test with? Please do not send sensitive living-person details.

## Success Criteria

Sprint 8 beta proof is good enough for Gate 2 prep when:

- At least 5 testers complete the follow-up.
- Average usefulness is 4+.
- At least 70% report one clear next action.
- No repeated P0/P1 issue prevents a beginner from using the plan.

## Issue Triage

Use this triage scale:

- **P0:** Blocks plan generation, page access, or email/print basics.
- **P1:** Generated plan is materially confusing, irrelevant, or missing an obvious first step for multiple testers.
- **P2:** Useful improvement, but not launch-blocking.
- **V2:** New feature idea outside MVP scope.

Only P0/P1 issues should enter Sprint 8 implementation work.

## Guardrails

Do not promise:

- Saved accounts.
- Exact emailed plan contents.
- Paid tier or premium features.
- AI/LLM personalization.
- Record lookup or record interpretation.

The MVP promise remains: "Tell us what you know. We'll tell you what to find next."
