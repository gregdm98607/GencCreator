# GenCreator

**Interactive Genealogy Research Planner** — a product of [Operation Granny Files](https://operationgrannyfiles.com) by Silver Sage Media.

> "Tell us what you know. We'll tell you what to find next."

## What Is GenCreator?

GenCreator helps beginner and intermediate genealogists create a prioritized research plan. Enter what you know about an ancestor — names, dates, locations, records you've already found — and GenCreator generates a step-by-step plan linking you to the databases, record collections, and educational resources you need next.
